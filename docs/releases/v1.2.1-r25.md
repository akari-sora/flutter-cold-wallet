## Scope and recovery limits / 範圍與復原限制

An owned emulator run using these signed APKs completed isolated ETH, mock USDC and BTC regtest signing flows. These results do not establish public-network behavior or physical-device security. 模擬器通過不代表公網或實體裝置安全已驗證。

Exporting an EVM request can leave a possible signature outstanding even after cancellation, timeout or a node's not-found response. This release does not implement general same-nonce replacement or force-abandon; incomplete historical EVM or Bitcoin records and interrupted restores can keep payment scopes blocked. Preserve original records and backups; do not clear history to bypass a block. 匯出交易請求後，取消、逾時或節點查無交易不會撤銷可能存在的簽章；不完整歷史或中斷還原可能使支付操作持續受阻。請保留原始紀錄和備份，勿清除歷史來解除限制。Further design and limits: [ADR-005](https://gitlab.com/aa22396584/flutter-cold-wallet/-/blob/v1.2.1/docs/ADR/ADR-005-mainnet-networks.md).
