# Google ML Kit terms delivery record

The Android dependency inventory pins bundled barcode scanning as `com.google.mlkit:barcode-scanning:17.3.0`. The project-specific AGPL section 7 linking/conveying permission is supplied in the archive root as `LICENSE-EXCEPTION`.

Google's official ML Kit terms page is <https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms> (page states last modified 2025-05-14) and incorporates the Google APIs Terms of Service at <https://developers.google.com/terms>. These are proprietary, mutable use terms rather than an open-source license text embedded in the r22 APK. This draft records the authoritative delivery locations instead of republishing Google's web page. At distribution time, license review must confirm the then-current terms and that this delivery treatment is sufficient.
